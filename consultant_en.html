If the total value of all your overseas assets exceeds $50,000 (bank accounts, shares, property and so on), you need to complete the Foreign Assets and Income schedule in your tax return. Completing the schedule is a reporting obligation — it doesn't mean those assets are taxed. Only income or gains they generate are included in your assessable income.
Australian tax residents who hold an asset for more than 12 months before selling are generally entitled to a 50% CGT discount — meaning only half the capital gain is added to your taxable income for that year. Assets sold within 12 months of purchase are taxed on the full gain at your marginal rate.
🏢 Small Business

Common questions about ABN, GST, employing staff and record-keeping

If you're running a business — even part-time or from home — you generally need an ABN. The ATO considers you a business if you intend to make a profit and repeat your activities regularly. Sole traders can register an ABN for free at abr.gov.au. Not having one when you should can mean 47% withholding tax on your invoices.
You must register for GST when your GST turnover reaches or is expected to reach A$75,000 in a 12-month period. For ride-share and taxi drivers, it's mandatory from your very first dollar of income. Once registered, you charge 10% GST on most goods and services and lodge a Business Activity Statement (BAS) — usually quarterly.
From the 2025–26 financial year, the Super Guarantee rate is 12% of an eligible employee's ordinary time earnings — the rate has now reached its final level. Super must be paid by the 28th day after each quarter ends. Late payment triggers the Superannuation Guarantee Charge, which includes the shortfall plus additional costs and is not tax-deductible.
Not automatically — the ATO and Fair Work have strict tests to determine whether someone is truly a contractor or is actually an employee. Factors include who controls the work, whether the worker can sub-contract, and whether they bring their own tools. Misclassifying an employee as a contractor can result in significant back-pay, super, and penalty obligations.
A Business Activity Statement (BAS) is how you report and pay GST, PAYG withholding, and other tax obligations to the ATO. Most GST-registered businesses lodge quarterly, within 28 days of the end of each quarter. Some small businesses can elect to lodge annually. You can lodge through myGov, the ATO's online services, or accounting software such as Xero or MYOB.
